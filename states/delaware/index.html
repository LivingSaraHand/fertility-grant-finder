Before you apply

  • Most grants pay your clinic or agency directly and won't cover treatment that has already started.
  • Many grants don't cover fertility medications. Ask your clinic about medication savings programs.
  • Confirm every date on the organization's own website. Dates marked "Needs confirmation" or "Not announced" can change.

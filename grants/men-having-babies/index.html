Men Having Babies

Gay Parenting Assistance Program (GPAP) · Discounts, free services, and small cash grants

Official application page Save or track in the finder
AwardStage I: discounts from participating IVF, surrogacy, egg donation, and legal providers. Stage II: free services and small cash grants (about $1,000,000 in free services awarded each year). Individual amounts aren't published.

WhereResidents of the USA, Canada, Taiwan, Israel, and several European countries have full access. Residents of some other countries can use Stage I only.

Review eligibility

  • For gay prospective parents; individuals or couples can apply.
  • Must be a Supporting Member of Men Having Babies in good standing.
  • Stage I is based on income and other criteria. Stage II applicants are typically expected to cover at least 30–40% of total U.S. journey costs.

What's covered and not covered

  • Stage I discounts are limited each calendar year.
  • Stage I recipients can't run parallel journeys with GPAP discounts.

Where this grant is available

Open to applicants in every U.S. state. See grants by state.

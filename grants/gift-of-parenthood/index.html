Gift of Parenthood

Gift of Parenthood Grant Program · Grant (the organization also runs a separate fundraising platform)

Official application page Save or track in the finder
AwardUp to $20,000 per award. The $80,000 figure sometimes quoted is the program's annual total, not one award.
Application fee$75 per cycle, or $250 for a Four-Cycle Pass (four submissions over 12 months). Early bird: pay $75 as three $25 payments until October 31, 2026. The fee is waived automatically if you raise $250 or more from at least two supporters on a Gift of Parenthood fundraiser.

Review eligibility

  • Age 18 or older, with documentation of your infertility journey and demonstrated financial need.
  • Good physical and mental health. At least one partner (or the individual applicant) employed.
  • Health insurance is not required.
  • Grants generally aren't given to people already receiving significant help from other charities.

What's covered and not covered

  • Can't be used for treatment that began before the award notification.
